## Design Context

### Users
AMC course assessors, classmates and the Meridian Capital team; visitors review the team's research, portfolio and members.

### Brand Personality
Measured, academic, precise. Preserve the team-selected globe/compass identity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Existing ivory and navy theme with cooler muted gold. The user explicitly chose DM Sans body text and plain white portrait backgrounds. Retain serif display headings.

### Design Principles
- Preserve names, identities and approved brand assets.
- Keep financial results factual and distinguish unpublished content.
- Use consistent portrait dimensions and readable names on mobile.
- Refine the existing website rather than introducing unrelated visual styles.
