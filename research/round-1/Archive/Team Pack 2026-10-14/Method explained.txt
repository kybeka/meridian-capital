HOW THE MODEL WORKS

1. Data
Yahoo Finance adjusted USD ETF prices are converted to complete month-end prices. Return = this adjusted price / previous adjusted price - 1. The current fitting window has 60 returns, October 2021-September 2026. Annual mean input = average monthly return x 12. Annual covariance = sample monthly covariance x 12. Inputs are historical estimates, not guaranteed returns. Exact sources and raw responses are in Data. Bloomberg has not replaced this data.

2. Portfolio rule
Constrained MVO maximizes estimated portfolio return, the sum of each weight times its annual mean input. Portfolio variance = weights transpose x annual covariance x weights; volatility is its square root. All eligible weights are jointly determined, rather than adjusted in fixed draft proportions. Python SLSQP generated the result. Excel contains the same objective and risk calculations; do not claim native Excel Solver generated the exported weights.

3. Requirements and policy choices
Challenge requirements: USD 100 million, long-only ETFs, no leverage, target annual ex-ante volatility 8% within a 7-9% band.
Our proposed policies: each eligible ETF <=35% capital; US broad stocks SPY+SCHD >=5%; emerging markets VWO+EEMV >=5%; DBC, VGSH and STIP >=5% each; VGSH+STIP >=20%; combined stock risk share <=65%; each ETF risk share <=40%. XLE is excluded; VWO remains eligible but has zero weight. These extra limits are team policy inputs, not rules imposed by the challenge and not automatically discovered by the optimizer.

4. Risk contribution
First calculate covariance matrix times weights. For each ETF, multiply that entry by its weight and divide by portfolio variance. The resulting risk shares sum to 100%. Capital weights and risk shares differ. MVO does not aim at equal risk. SPY and DBC each reach approximately 40% risk, and VGSH reaches its 5% capital floor. The other weights balance the objective and constraints. Removing XLE does not remove commodity energy exposure from DBC.

5. Equal weight and risk parity
The Checkpoint 2 comparison uses the six selected ETFs and the same current 60-month covariance. Equal weight is exactly 1/6 per ETF; its risk shares are unequal. Risk parity chooses capital weights so each ETF contributes 1/6 of portfolio risk. Use full precision for calculations and round only for display. Current estimated volatility is 8.00% for MVO, 7.80% for equal weight and 3.75% for risk parity. This natural fully invested risk-parity benchmark is below the required band; it is not another recommended allocation.

6. Fixed SAA backtest
Monthly rebalancing restores the same target weights after price changes. It does not calculate new optimized targets every quarter. The main report applies today’s proposed weights retrospectively to November 2016-September 2026. The group reviewed historical results while selecting the universe and model; no historical segment was an untouched team-selection holdout. Earlier rolling MVO tests remain in the research archive only.

7. Costs and performance
Gross monthly return = sum(weight x ETF return). Drifted weight after a month = target weight x (1 + ETF return) / (1 + gross portfolio return). Turnover = sum of absolute target-minus-drifted weights, counting purchases and sales without dividing by two. Inception turnover is 1. Net return = (1 - 0.001 x turnover) x (1 + gross return) - 1.
CAGR compounds net monthly returns. Annual realized volatility = sample monthly standard deviation x sqrt(12). Sharpe = mean monthly net excess return / standard deviation of monthly net portfolio return x sqrt(12). Risk-free monthly proxy = last available FRED DGS1MO percentage yield before the month / 100 x calendar days / 365. This is a yield-accrual proxy, not an observed T-bill total-return index.
Drawdown = wealth / highest wealth so far - 1; report the minimum, including starting capital and month-end observations. Historical monthly 95% VaR = minus the 5th percentile of monthly net returns, with linear interpolation. The current normal VaR, 3.80% or USD 3.798 million, instead uses 1.64485 x current estimated annual volatility / sqrt(12), assuming zero mean and normal returns. It is not the 3.68% historical VaR.

8. Validation and uncertainty
Separate tests fit weights before November 2016 and November 2021, then freeze those fitted targets for subsequent historical returns. These exclude evaluation returns from the numeric fit, but the universe and policies were chosen retrospectively. They are conditional historical validation, not untouched holdouts and not tests of the exact proposed weights. The current portfolio remains unchanged. Exact trials known from retained files are listed in Results/research_variations.csv; counts overlap, and an exact independent whole-team total is not known. Teammates must add any additional tests before calling that ledger exhaustive. There is no statistically determined or team-agreed Sharpe haircut.
